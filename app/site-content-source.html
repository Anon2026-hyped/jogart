Biogas and waste-to-energy

From organic waste to clean energy.

Organic waste is a valuable resource. Through anaerobic digestion, innovative technologies and strategic partnerships, we support the recovery of energy from food waste, agricultural residues, animal manure and other suitable biodegradable materials.

  1. Feedstock management

    Collection, segregation and preparation of suitable organic waste for biogas production.

  2. Anaerobic digestion

    Converting biodegradable waste into biogas through controlled biological processes that operate without oxygen.

  3. Renewable energy recovery

    Using biogas for electricity and heating, with potential upgrading into renewable natural gas (RNG).

  4. Digestate and resource recovery

    Responsible treatment and beneficial use of suitable by-products as soil amendments or fertilizers, subject to quality and regulatory requirements.
