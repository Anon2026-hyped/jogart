Biogas & waste-to-energy

From organic waste to clean energy.

Through innovative waste management technologies and strategic partnerships, JOGAT Green Inc. supports the recovery of energy from food waste, agricultural residues, animal manure and other suitable biodegradable waste streams — reducing landfill dependence and greenhouse gas emissions.

Discuss a biogas project
  1. 1

    Feedstock management

    Collection, segregation and preparation of suitable organic waste for biogas production.

  2. 2

    Anaerobic digestion

    Converting biodegradable waste into biogas through controlled biological processes that operate without oxygen.

  3. 3

    Renewable energy recovery

    Using biogas for electricity generation, heating and potential upgrading into renewable natural gas (RNG).

  4. 4

    Digestate & resource recovery

    Responsible treatment and beneficial use of suitable by-products as soil amendments or fertilizers, subject to quality and regulatory requirements.

Biogas project development & advisory

Project planning, feasibility assessment, technology evaluation and sustainability consulting for organic waste-to-energy initiatives.

  • Lower GHG emissions
  • Less organic waste landfilled
  • Renewable energy generation
